WAREHOUSE STAFF TRAINING CHECKLIST

Use this checklist while training warehouse staff on the warehouse system.
Mark each item after you explain it and let the trainee try it.

==================================================
1. BASIC STARTUP
==================================================

[ ] Open the warehouse app
[ ] Explain what this app is responsible for
[ ] Explain that warehouse is the source of truth for stock

==================================================
